## What happened this session

### 1. Ported staleness alert + 12h cooldown to espadavilla (first time)
Espadavilla's failure-monitor previously had NO dead-man's switch. Golfvilla had one
but it re-alerted hourly during outages (causing WhatsApp floods). This session added
the full pattern — with cooldown — to espadavilla.

**File changed:** `src/lib/monitoring/failure-monitor.ts`

New constants added:
```typescript
const STALENESS_ALERT_HOURS = Number(process.env.STALENESS_ALERT_HOURS ?? 3);
const STALENESS_COOLDOWN_HOURS = 12;
```

New functions:
- `hoursSinceLastRun()` — most recent non-`stale_alert_sent` row age in hours
- `hoursSinceLastStaleAlert()` — hours since last cooldown marker

`runFailureMonitor()` now: detects silence at 3h, sends WhatsApp, inserts a
`run_type='stale_alert_sent'` marker row, then won't re-alert for 12h.

**Behavior:** First alert at 3h of silence; max one re-alert per 12h
(at most 2 messages/day during a sustained Vercel cron outage).

### 2. Changed publishing cadence: 1x/week → 2x/week
**File changed:** `vercel.json`

| Cron | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `draft-weekly-post` | `0 13 * * 4` (Thu only) | `0 13 * * 1,4` (Mon + Thu) |
| `blog-pipeline-worker` | `*/2 13-16 * * 4` | `*/2 13-16 * * 1,4` |

`post-refresh` and `gsc-topics` remain weekly (Thursday) — no need to double data-gathering.

**Next runs:** Mon 2026-06-30 13:00 UTC and Thu 2026-07-03 13:00 UTC.
